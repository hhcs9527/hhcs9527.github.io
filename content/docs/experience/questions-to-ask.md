## Join in to a new team
### Contribution
- How does the review process works?
  - Directly review / assigned then review
- How to merge the code? any tradition?
  - squash / rebase merge?
  - Who to merge?
### Techinal
- How does the new feature / design flow works?
  - Where is the design docs?
### Reach out
- Who are the key stakeholders I should know?
